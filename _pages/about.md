Hi! My name is Stephen (pronounced STEH-fən) and I am a Ph.D. student in computer science at **[UC Berkeley](https://bair.berkeley.edu/)** supervised by Prof. **[Yun S. Song](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~yss/)**. I completed my undergraduate studies at **[McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/)** in my hometown of Montreal, Canada 🇨🇦.

My research focuses on machine learning approaches for protein sequence and structure. I am broadly interested in developing deep probabilistic models of **protein evolution and dynamics**. Recently, my work has centered on learning representations of antibody selection during affinity maturation [[1]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18982), and building generative models for protein conformation sampling [[2]](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f4901fd0bd9a42f840eabfddf4e7f4f9-Abstract-Conference.html), [[3]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24203).

Previously, I dabbled in small molecule generative models [[4]](https://openreview.net/forum?id=H73QKn7P2o), [[5]](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/948d8ba4e30c8c3a800cf436b31f376e-Abstract-Conference.html) and agentic benchmarking for autonomous scientific discovery [[6]](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/a23760ba51036d530a2656c3835f826c-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html).
